Kurtain Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-13

Kurtain is an Android app that covers the Reels screen and the Explore recommendation feed in Instagram, at the user's own choice. Kurtain is not affiliated with Instagram or Meta.

Personal data we collect: none

Kurtain has no accounts and no servers. It does not send any data off your device.

How Kurtain uses the Accessibility Service

Kurtain uses Android's AccessibilityService API only to decide whether the Instagram screen in front of you is Reels or the Explore recommendation feed. The service receives events only from the Instagram app (com.instagram.android); Android does not deliver other apps' screens to it.

Kurtain reads the structure of the screen (element types, layout, resource IDs). It does not keep the text on the screen — messages, posts, comments, or account names. Each snapshot is discarded right after the decision.

What stays on your device

Your consent choice, your blocking settings, and the result of the last self-test. Uninstalling Kurtain deletes them.

Third parties

Kurtain contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, and no crash reporting.
